# FixProof external validation packet

Use this only after a willing appliance owner or repair professional is available. Do not claim validation from the fictional demo or the local AI evaluation.

## Five-minute owner test

Give the tester the local URL without explaining the interface. Ask them to use a fictional issue if they prefer. Observe without coaching:

1. Can they tell that the experience is simulated and supports only the three listed exact models?
2. Can they find the exact-model requirement and understand why it matters?
3. Can they ask for a check, inspect the source, and distinguish a suggestion from an attempted check?
4. After recording an outcome and reloading, can they identify what happened and what remains?
5. Does the handover save useful explanation compared with manual plus notes? What fact is missing?

Record time to first supported check, source opened or not, outcome recorded or not, reload success, handover understood, and one verbatim observation with consent. Do not collect names, addresses, serial numbers or warranty documents.

## Repair-professional review

Show only a fictional handover. Ask:

- Which fields would help you decide the next service action?
- Which fields are ambiguous or risky?
- Does “not yet tested” read differently from “still wet”?
- What would make you distrust the record?
- Would receiving this before a call save time, create work, or make no difference?

## Decision gate

Continue expanding appliance coverage only if at least two independent testers complete the core path without coaching and identify a concrete advantage over manual plus notes. Rework the flow if they confuse suggestions with completed work or treat it as a diagnosis. Park the concept if the handover adds no useful information for both an owner and a professional.

This packet is ready for remote screen-share or in-person use. No outreach, recording or data collection has been performed.
